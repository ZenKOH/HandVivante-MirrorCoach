# Clinical content and governance notes

## RMHT premise

MirrorCoach operationalises a three-part clinical premise:

1. mirror visual feedback;
2. synchronous robotic movement of the affected hand;
3. explicit motor intention, imagery, attention, and attempted movement.

The platform treats this as an evidence-informed mechanistic and implementation model, not a guarantee of superiority over dose-matched conventional therapy.

## Product-screening fields represented in the prototype

The supplied HandVivante training material lists:

- hand motor impairment related to neuro-muscular-skeletal injury;
- Modified Ashworth Scale below 3;
- stable physiological condition;
- MMSE 24 or above;
- Brunnstrom Recovery Stage of the arm 2 or above.

The supplied material also lists contraindication examples including poor cognitive ability, the specified mental/neurological concern such as convulsions, wrist musculoskeletal disease that cannot withstand external forces, skin disorders, and cerebellar stroke. These items must be verified against the current market-specific IFU and local clinical governance before use.

## Dose model

The standard guided session is 30 minutes:

- 3 minutes safety/readiness;
- 4 minutes calibration/familiarisation;
- 5 minutes priming;
- 15 minutes high-repetition practice;
- 3 minutes transfer/cooldown.

The four-week pathway generally progresses tolerance and range before speed, task complexity, volitional demand, and reduced assistance.

## Outcome framework

MirrorCoach records scores and context for measures such as FMA-UE, ARAT, Box and Block Test, Motor Activity Log, and patient-specific functional goals. It does not reproduce proprietary assessment forms or replace authorised scoring materials.

## Safety principles

Immediate stop categories include:

- escalating or disproportionate pain;
- skin or neurovascular change;
- sustained spasticity/clonus or unsafe range;
- obstruction, loss of synchrony, unexpected movement, warning indication, unusual sound, odour, or heat.

The production version should link each event to local escalation procedures, device identifiers, software/firmware versions, assessor identity, and audit history.
